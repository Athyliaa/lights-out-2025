    01

    The fastest circuit racing on earth.

    Formula 1 is the top level of single-seater racing. Ten teams each design and build their own car, run two drivers, and travel the world from March to December. Every Grand Prix pays out points, and at the end of the year there are two titles: one for drivers, one for teams.

    02

    Three days. One Sunday that counts.

    Most rounds follow the standard format. Six of 2025's 24 weekends were Sprint weekends, with less practice and an extra short race on Saturday.

    03

    Every point counts.

    The top ten drivers in every Grand Prix earn points. In 2025, the championship was decided by an incredibly narrow margin, with just two points separating the winner from the runner-up.
